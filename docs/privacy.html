Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Noafel Auto content ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What data the app handles

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok or Instagram when the owner authorizes the app for the owner's own account.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

How it is used

Tokens are used only to publish the owner's scheduled videos. They are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared, sold or used for advertising or analytics.

Other users

The app does not collect, store or process personal data from any other person.

Retention and deletion

Published videos are removed from the app's storage after publishing. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the TikTok or Instagram settings, and can delete stored tokens at any time.

Contact

Questions can be sent by opening an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
